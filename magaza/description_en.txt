🌍 The fate of the world is in your hands!

Bayrak Savaşları (Flag Wars) is a turn-based world strategy game with 136 countries and 520 cities. Pick a country, build your army, grow your cities, forge alliances and become the strongest nation on Earth!

• Online with friends: 1–4 players join with a 4-digit room code
• Land attacks, naval landings, airborne drops, missiles, weather and fronts
• Pacts, trade, ultimatums and peace; control straits and toll ships — or close them and risk a global crisis
• Buildings, research, World Wonders and a Science Victory
• Keep your people happy and win elections; make tough calls in random events
• BALL NEWS 24: funny anchors recap every day
• Three eras: Ancient, Ottoman era and Modern day
• Endless mode, difficulty levels, an adaptive AI, quests and achievements

No ads · No account needed

All countries, events and news in the game are fictional satire.
